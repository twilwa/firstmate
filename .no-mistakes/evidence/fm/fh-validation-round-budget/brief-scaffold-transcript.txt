=== gate rules in a no-mistakes ship DOD (fm-focused) ===
137:Before starting a run, fetch the base branch and rebase onto it when it has moved since your worktree was cut, resolving any conflicts yourself: a run submitted on a stale base leaves the merge-conflict repair to the pipeline's CI step.
148:These firstmate-specific rules layer on top of that guidance:
152:- Decline a review finding with `--step review --action approve`, never `--action skip --findings <id>`: a named review skip marks the whole Review step skipped, and Push then refuses for lack of a review-approved head.
153:  Approve declines only when every open finding is declined; when you decline some findings while fixing others, name each decline in `--ignore <id>` on the same `--action fix`, because an approve drops the fixes you selected.
154:- Approve a review coverage park only with a stated reason, written in a status line to firstmate before the approve - the unverified files lie outside your own change, such as a stale base widening the diff, or after one `complete-coverage` round scoped to them - because a bare approve ships unreviewed code.
155:- Stop selecting fixes once review rounds stop converging - about 4 fix rounds on one step, or a gate finding an earlier round introduced - and report `needs-decision` to firstmate with the still-open findings instead of continuing to iterate.
